# research-projects

Open-source tools, data, and source codes for advanced engineering, software development, physics, and alternative energy systems.

This repository serves as a centralized hub for all theoretical frameworks, mathematical models, firmware developments, and software architectures developed across multiple engineering disciplines.

---

## 🚀 Active Projects & Research Fields

### 1. Artificial Intelligence & Software Systems
* **Jarvis AI Assistant:** A custom LLM-based autonomous personal assistant engineered to streamline research workflows, manage technical documentation, and automate environment configurations.
* **Reklam Pazarı Application:** A mobile marketplace platform featuring a high-performance swiping user interface designed for dynamic digital ad-space monetization.
* **Team-Building Social Platform:** A separate, specialized networking app built to facilitate structural team-building and milestone management for engineering competitions and startups.

### 2. Advanced Defense Systems & Armor Technology
* **Composite Armor & Structural Ballistics:** Theoretical modeling and simulation framework for advanced vehicle/body armor. Focuses on shockwave propagation, energy dissipation mechanics in non-homogenous composite materials, and high-velocity impact analysis using mechanical hydrocodes.

### 3. Solid-State Climate Control
* **Magneto-Caloric Hybrid Regenerative Cycle (MCHR):** Next-generation solid-state cooling system that completely eliminates gas compressors. Integrates an energy-recovering LC resonant driver network (70% power reduction), cascade porous matrices with graded Curie temperatures, and magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) ferro-nanofluid loops.

### 4. Aerospace & Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)
* **Autonomous UAV Systems (TÜBİTAK / QEAS):** Flight telemetry logging, structural aerodynamics optimization data, and custom embedded control architectures developed for high-efficiency fixed-wing and multirotor UAV flight dynamics.

### 5. Electric Vehicles & Embedded Systems
* **EV Powertrain & Battery Management (Team BORA - Teknofest):** Full-stack firmware development, thermal analysis, and cell-balancing algorithms for high-rate Lithium Titanate Oxide (LTO) battery packs, coupled with low-level STM32 C/C++ motor control firmware.

### 6. Alternative Energy & Nuclear Physics
* **Microwave Plasma Hydrogen Production:** Open-source datasets, control parameters, and numerical simulations focused on ultra-efficient hydrocarbon cracking via stabilized microwave plasma generation.
* **Thorium Fuel Cycles:** Theoretical core neutron flux calculations and passive safety loop designs for molten salt thorium reactors.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Tools Used

* **Software & AI:** Python, custom LLM pipelines, Mobile App Frameworks (Kotlin/Native & Cross-Platform frameworks).
* **Hardware & Firmware:** STM32 Ecosystem (C/C++ HAL/LL), Real-Time Motor Control, Circuit Board Architecture, and I2C/SPI/CAN protocols.
* **CAD & Physics Simulators:** SolidWorks (CAD modeling & Thermal FEA), LS-DYNA / Autodyn (Civilian impact/ballistic simulations).
